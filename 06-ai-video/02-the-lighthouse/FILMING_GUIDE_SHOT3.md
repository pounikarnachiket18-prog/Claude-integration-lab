# Filming Guide: Shot 3 (on your iPad)

**The shot:** old hands turn a brass key, and a lamp glows warm.
**Final length:** 4 seconds. **You'll film:** about 10 seconds, 3 times.
**Cost:** free.

---

## 1. What you need

| Item | Easy substitutes |
|---|---|
| **A key** | Any metal key: house key in a door, drawer or cupboard lock. Old or brass-looking is best. |
| **A warm light that turns on** | A bedside lamp with a switch · a warm LED candle · a phone flashlight behind orange paper |
| **A dark room** | Evening, curtains closed, main lights off |
| **Something to hold the iPad still** | A stack of books, a tripod, or leaning it against a box |

⚠️ If you use a real candle, keep it away from paper, curtains and sleeves.

---

## 2. Set up the iPad (once)

Open **Settings → Camera**:

| Setting | Choose | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Record Video** | **1080p at 30 fps** | Matches our film exactly, and keeps the file small |
| **Formats** | **Most Compatible** | Saves as H.264, which every tool can read |
| **HDR Video** (if shown) | **Off** | HDR makes colors look strange after editing |

---

## 3. Set up the scene

```
        [ lamp ]                 ← off at first
           |
   (key) [lock]  ← your hands here
                                 ~30–40 cm away
      [ iPad on books ]          ← held SIDEWAYS (landscape)
```

1. **Hold the iPad sideways (landscape).** Our film is wide, so sideways is essential.
2. **Use the 1× camera**, not 0.5×. It gives a more natural "film" look.
3. **Get close:** hands and key should fill most of the screen.
4. **Put the iPad down** on books or a tripod. **Handheld footage shakes.**
5. **Make it dark:** one small light only (a dim lamp nearby, or light from the hallway) so you can just see your hands.

---

## 4. Lock the camera (the pro trick)

iPads automatically brighten dark scenes, which would ruin the mood.

1. **Tap and hold** on your hands until **"AE/AF LOCK"** appears.
   This freezes the focus and brightness.
2. **Drag the little sun ☀️ down** a bit, so the picture looks **darker** than feels right.
   *Dark and moody is the goal. It's easier to brighten later than to fix overexposed footage.*

---

## 5. The action (count it out loud in your head)

| Count | What you do |
|---|---|
| **1, 2** | Hands still, on the key. *(Lamp is OFF)* |
| **3, 4** | **Slowly** turn the key. Slower than feels natural. |
| **5** | Lamp turns ON (switch it with your other hand, or ask a helper) |
| **6, 7, 8** | Hands rest still in the warm light |
| **9, 10** | Stop recording |

**Film it 3 times** (these are called **takes**). Try one slower, one with your hands closer.

---

## 6. Check before you send

- [ ] Sideways (landscape)?
- [ ] No shaking?
- [ ] Dark at the start, warm light at the end?
- [ ] Hands in focus?
- [ ] About 10 seconds long?

**color** it to match the film (cold shadows, warm lamp), and add **grain** and
**letterbox bars**, so it blends in with the shots made with code.
