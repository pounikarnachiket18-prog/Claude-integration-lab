How does a nuclear bomb actually explode? ☢️ It all happens in about a millionth of a second.

1️⃣ A neutron hits a uranium-235 nucleus
2️⃣ The nucleus splits in two (fission), releasing energy and 2–3 new neutrons
3️⃣ Those neutrons split more atoms: 1 → 2 → 4 → 8… a chain reaction
4️⃣ The pieces weigh slightly less than the original atom, and that missing mass becomes energy (E = mc²)
5️⃣ Result: a fireball, a shockwave and a mushroom cloud

Used in war only twice, at Hiroshima and Nagasaki in 1945. Understanding its power is why the world works to never use it again. 🕊️
