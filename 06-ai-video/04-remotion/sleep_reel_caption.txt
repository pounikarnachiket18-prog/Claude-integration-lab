Can't sleep? Try these 5 tips tonight 🌙

1. Same bedtime, every day (weekends too)
2. Screens off 1 hour before bed
3. Keep your room cool and dark (~18°C / 65°F)
4. No caffeine after 2 PM
5. Breathe 4 · 7 · 8: in for 4, hold for 7, out for 8

Save this for tonight and send it to someone who needs better sleep 💤

These are general wellness tips, not medical advice. If sleep problems keep going, talk to a doctor.
